Navigating the PG Apartment: Essential Do’s and Don’ts

By Pranav Sharma

Lifestyle & Selected Writing

Living in a Paying Guest (PG) apartment can be an exciting experience, offering a blend of independence and community. However, maintaining harmony in a PG requires some consideration and etiquette.

Even if you are a seasoned PG resident or new, here is a guide to help you navigate the do’s and don’ts of living in a PG apartment.

Do’s

Communicate Openly

Good communication is the key to a smooth living experience. If staying with a roommate, always communicate about cleanliness, guests, and shared expenses early on. These should be the ground rules that you both set up at the start.

Do Respect Quiet Hours

You start living outside your home for studies, so respect the quiet hours, i.e., studying times for others also. Always remember if you don’t want to be disturbed while concentrating, there will be others also who want the peace.

Do Label Your Stuff

In the shared PG, always label your stuff to avoid mixing them up with other people's belongings. This is especially useful in shared spaces such as the kitchen.

Don’ts

Don’t Ignore Shared Responsibilities

Living in a PG means sharing certain responsibilities. Don't leave cleaning, maintenance, or other common tasks entirely to your roommates.

Don’t Disturb Others

Remember that everyone has their own routine. Avoid unnecessarily disturbing your roommates, especially when they are studying, working, sleeping, or simply looking for some personal space.

Don’t Take Other People’s Things

Respect other people's belongings. Always ask before using something that belongs to your roommate or another resident.

Final Thought

A PG can become a comfortable home away from home when everyone respects each other's space, responsibilities, and routines. Communication, consideration, and basic etiquette can make shared living much easier and more enjoyable.
